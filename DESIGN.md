## Direction
 
Hand-made, editorial, a bit cheeky. Closer to a well-loved magazine or a small Soho restaurant than to an ed-tech app.
 
Reference points the founder gave: Ben's Cookies, Quo Vadis (London), JSTOR, Notion, instant.ai. The instant.ai logo, a crude marker doodle of a cat, is the main reference for the *feel* of the mark. Amural's cat is an original drawing; it must not copy instant.ai's wizard hat, wand or star.
 
**Rejected directions** (so we don't revisit them):
- A geometric "A" built from coloured tiles: too infographic, too uniform.
- An evenly traced line drawing of a cat with a book: too clean and standard, lacking character.
- A multicolour palette in the logo: the logo stays monochrome.
- A dark theme as the default: the brand is light-themed.
## Logo
 
**The mark:** a loose, black-marker doodle of a **cat's face in wonky round glasses, peeking over an open book**, with a few "idea" spark marks around it.
- Lumpy head outline, open at the bottom; dot eyes behind the glasses; a "w" mouth; whiskers; a crude open book with a spine and a couple of text lines.
- Strokes are uneven and wobbly, with round ends, like a felt-tip marker. Imperfection is the point.
- The canvas has three takes with different wobble; pick the one with the best expression.
- **Final artwork:** draw the chosen take by hand with a real marker, scan it and vectorise it. A hand-drawn original gives the true wobble that a generated version only approximates.
**The wordmark:** "Amural" set in Philibert Italic Rush, upper and lower case.
 
**Lockups:**
- Primary: the cat to the left of the wordmark, with the tagline underneath.
- Stacked: the cat above the wordmark.
**Small sizes:** at 96px and below, use the simplified cat: no glasses, no page lines or extra sparks, thicker strokes. App icon 220px; favicons 48px and 32px.
 
**Rules:**
- Always one colour: marker on paper. Never multicolour, gradients or effects.
- Colour belongs to the content (lesson covers, subject tags), never to the mark.

## Typography
 
**Display: Philibert Italic Rush** (OSP foundry, Brussels)
- Based on the lettering of old French road signs for hamlets and localities. A chunky serif italic with a magazine voice.
- Use for the wordmark, headlines, the tagline and pull quotes.
- Licence: SIL Open Font License 1.1, per OSP's repository README. The font file's metadata carries an older "Hammerfonts, 2000. All rights reserved" line; check this before registering the logo as a trademark.
- Covers Latin script only, so Greek and Bulgarian headlines fall back to the body font, Geologica.
- Caveat found in the font file: nearly every accented letter has wrong spacing (it leaves a gap or runs into the next letter) and ă, ș and ț are missing. The site sets a heading in Philibert only when it can draw every character, and falls back to Geologica semibold otherwise (`App\Support\DisplayFont`). A complete version of the font would remove the fallback.
**Text: Geologica** (Monokrom, Norway; SIL OFL 1.1)
- Body copy and interface. A variable font (weight 100 to 900), self-hosted, with Latin, Latin Extended, Greek, Cyrillic and Cyrillic Extended, so every EU language (plus Ukrainian) renders in one family. Its regular weight runs heavy, so body text is set at weight 350 and interface labels at 450.
**Considered but not chosen:** Crickx / Publi Fluor (OSP: cut-vinyl shop lettering from Brussels) and Daubenton (Velvetyne: a revival of the engraved letters on the façade of the Natural History Museum's evolution gallery in Paris). Both are free under the OFL and could serve as occasional accents.

## Name note
 
*Amural* reads as "a mural": a shared wall many hands paint. In Spanish, *amurallado* means "walled in", which is harmless but worth knowing.
